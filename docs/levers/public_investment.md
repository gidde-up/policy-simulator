# Lever: public investment

A public investment programme of a chosen size (% of GDP) injected into
final demand, either:
- **broad**: allocated across sectors by the country's own gross fixed
  capital formation (GFCF) product composition from the ICIO final
  demand block, or
- **targeted**: the full amount into one chosen sector.

Pure final-demand accounting through the Leontief system; no behavioural
parameters. Fiscal cost = the injection, carrying the financing-drag
toggle (default on, tax-financed) like sector support, so gross and net
effects can be compared.
